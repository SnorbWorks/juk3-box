Juk3box

A jukebox for your home that plays your Apple Music library.

The iPhone app

Juk3box (Juk3box Helper) is the iPhone companion for a juk3box jukebox. You pick songs on the cabinet; the app shares your Apple Music library with it and plays the songs you pick, sending the sound to the jukebox over AirPlay. The app needs a juk3box on your home network to do anything.

Getting started

  1. Turn on your jukebox. A new one shows its own Wi-Fi network and password on its screen.
  2. In the app, tap Set up a new jukebox and scan the code on the jukebox's screen, or join its Wi-Fi from Settings › Wi-Fi.
  3. Follow the steps on the jukebox to put it on your home Wi-Fi.
  4. Back in the app, type the 4-digit code the jukebox shows, allow Apple Music, and choose the jukebox as the speaker in the AirPlay menu.
