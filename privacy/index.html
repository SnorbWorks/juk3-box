Privacy Policy

Juk3box (Juk3box Helper) for iPhone · Last updated October 26, 2026

The short version: the app collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It talks only to your own jukebox on your own home network. Nothing it reads is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app uses, and where it goes

  • Your Apple Music library (song, album, artist and playlist names, and artwork). This is shared with your jukebox on your home network, so the jukebox can show your songs and play the ones you pick. It goes nowhere else.
  • Local network. Used to find your jukebox and talk to it.
  • Bluetooth. Your jukebox can wake the app over Bluetooth when someone picks a song, so it can play while your phone is locked. Only your paired jukebox is used.
  • Camera. Used only to scan the setup code on a new jukebox's screen. No pictures are saved or sent anywhere.
  • Location. iOS only tells an app the name of your Wi-Fi network if it has location permission. The app reads the network name once during setup, to suggest it to your jukebox. Your location itself is never read, saved or sent.
  • Your phone's name on the jukebox. Whatever you type under "This phone" is sent to your jukebox so it can show who is connected.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps a pairing key for your jukebox in the iPhone's Keychain, and a few settings (which jukebox you use, your phone's name on it). Deleting the app removes them.

Your jukebox

The jukebox is a device you own, in your home. What the app shares with it stays on it. The jukebox does not send your library or listening to us.

Apple

Songs play through Apple Music, using Apple's own frameworks. Apple's handling of your Apple Music account is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
